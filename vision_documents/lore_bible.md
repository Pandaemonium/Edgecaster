# Edgecaster Lore Bible

## 1. How To Use This Document

This is the main setting reference for world truths and lore cohesion.

Use these labels while reading:

- `Hard canon`: world truth the setting should treat as real unless explicitly revised later
- `Common knowledge`: what an ordinary educated person is likely to know or believe
- `Legend or contested memory`: widely told, variably interpreted, or politically disputed
- `Hidden truth`: real or planned-to-be-real material that should not be front-loaded to the player
- `Possible future path`: an idea the project likes, but has not committed to as canon

Not every statement needs a label, but the distinction matters whenever agents are writing quests, dialogue, books, codex text, or faction reactions.

## 2. World At A Glance

`Hard canon`

Edgecaster takes place on a large continent containing multiple powers, peoples, traditions, ruins, and ecological regions. The world is old, politically layered, and visibly shaped by magic.

The main antagonistic force is demonic. Demons are responsible for much of the world's deepest corruption, horror, predation, and occult instability. That does not mean all dark things are demonic, nor that every region should feel demon-saturated.

## 3. The Astean Empire

`Hard canon`

The Astean Empire was founded roughly 800 years ago by Czar Asteus. At its height it ruled the full continent for about 250 years. It later receded, and today holds roughly a third of the continent while many regions operate as independent kingdoms, city-states, or other polities.

The empire's long legacy is still visible in:

- roads
- ruins
- institutions
- universities
- magical infrastructure
- the Rune Gate network

`Legend or contested memory`

To imperial loyalists, Asteus is a sacred founder and civilizing hero. To scholars, he is often remembered as a foundational mage, geometer, and state-builder. To others he is remembered as a conqueror, dictator, or simply ancient history.

## 4. Edgecasters

`Hard canon`

Edgecasters were an ancient order whose magic drew directly from the body's own chakra geometry. Their power was not merely learned technique or priestly favor. It was an inherent capacity to channel fractal force through the body's energy topology.

The order was founded by Asteus and served as some combination of:

- scholars
- engineers
- commanders
- magical specialists
- state agents

Demons recognized the order as a major threat and hunted Edgecasters toward extinction.

`Common knowledge`

Many people know some version of the story that Edgecasters fought demons, shaped old wonders, and belonged to a lost age of great magic. The level of detail varies wildly. For many ordinary people, Edgecasters are historical and half-mythic rather than immediate.

`Hidden truth`

The player is the last known living Edgecaster. This should not be treated as a casual early-game fact dump.

## 5. Rune Gates, Crystals, And Infrastructure

`Hard canon`

Rune Gates are large stone arches inscribed with stable geometric patterns. They transport travelers along fixed Julia-set orbit links rather than offering arbitrary teleportation.

Two crystal types underpin persistent rune magic:

- `Coherence crystals` maintain pattern integrity.
- `Resonance crystals` provide ongoing power.

Neither crystal type simply "runs out" under normal use. Persistent rune structures remain active unless damaged, dismantled, or deprived of their crystals.

The Rune Gate network remains active in the present day, though it is ancient, only partly understood, and unevenly maintained.

`Common knowledge`

Rune Gates are widely known. Ordinary people may not understand their mathematics, but they understand what gates are, what they are used for, and that they are old and important.

## 6. Crystals And The Dwarven Alliance

`Hard canon`

Asteus predicted where major crystal deposits could be found. This helped enable an alliance with the dwarves, who mined those deposits and shared in the wealth and infrastructure boom of the Astean golden age.

The richest veins are long depleted. New growth still occurs underground, but in smaller and slower-forming deposits. Crystals remain strategically important and valuable, but they are no longer a simple source of easy continental-scale wealth.

## 7. Demons

`Hard canon`

Demons are the setting's main antagonistic force. Their motives differ in style but not in ultimate direction: they seek power.

Demons gain power primarily by consuming chakras, including living body parts, active structures, items, or other chakra-bearing entities. Active chakras are more valuable than inert ones, which helps explain why demonic corruption often matures slowly before turning openly violent.

Demons can be:

- horrifying
- charismatic
- grotesque
- eerie
- cute in presentation
- seductive
- bureaucratic
- absurd in a way that is still threatening

What unifies them is appetite, leverage, corruption, and hunger for power.

`Hard canon`

The named Demon Lords from NetHack exist in Edgecaster as canonical demon lords. Their Edgecaster versions should be rewritten in this world's terms. Their powers, minions, domains, and relationships should not be copied over mechanically from NetHack.

## 8. Demon Taxonomy And Public Understanding

`Hard canon`

There is a real demon taxonomy in-world. Top scholars, occult specialists, and serious demonologists should be able to distinguish major demon kinds and give meaningful classifications.

`Common knowledge`

Ordinary folklore is messy. Commoners may mix demon stories together, confuse one kind for another, or repeat incorrect lore about powers and weaknesses.

This distinction matters:

- scholarly writing can be precise
- village warnings can be wrong
- cult propaganda can be self-serving

## 9. Magic And Chakras

`Hard canon`

Many magical traditions exist on the continent. Most of them connect in some way to chakra logic: energy flows through connected nodes, whether or not a given culture uses that exact vocabulary.

Edgecaster magic is special because the practitioner's own body is the direct geometric instrument. Other traditions may rely on ritual, objects, bloodline, cult practice, alchemy, vows, divination, or external structures while still participating in the broader chakra substrate of the world.

## 10. The Julia-Set World

`Hard canon`

The continent's geography is shaped by Julia-set structure. Terrain, climate, and biome distribution are not merely inspired by fractals; fractal mathematics is part of the world's underlying reality.

Asteus discovered enough of this structure to build the Rune Gate network and reshape trade, state power, and scholarship around it.

`Common knowledge`

Most people do not understand the mathematics. They simply know that the world has strange geography, old gates, and places where reality behaves in unnerving ways.

## 11. Corruption And Dimensional Distortion

`Possible future path`

One possible deeper explanation is that demonic corruption introduces quaternionic or octonionic distortions into the Julia field, bending it through additional dimensions and producing warped terrain and unstable reality.

This idea is not committed canon yet. It should be treated as a promising design direction rather than a settled fact. Before it is promoted, it should prove worthwhile for gameplay, performance, and player legibility.

## 12. What Ordinary People Know

`Common knowledge`

### Rune Gates

Most people know Rune Gates are ancient travel infrastructure. They know the gates are powerful, useful, and not fully understood.

### Demons

Everyone knows demons exist in some degree. What they do not know reliably is the full range of demon kinds, powers, habits, and countermeasures.

### Edgecasters

Edgecasters are widely known as a historical or legendary order. Many people have heard that they fought demons and helped shape the old magical world. Only some people know detailed stories, and those details may be inaccurate, romanticized, or politically slanted.

### Czar Asteus

Asteus is a major historical figure almost everywhere, but the emotional interpretation changes by culture, class, and ideology.

## 13. Hidden Truth And Spoiler Discipline

`Hidden truth`

The player's eventual relationship to large-scale bodies, macro-entities, and deeper observer-scale possibilities should remain surprising. Early content should feel local, embodied, and human-scaled.

This does not mean the world itself is simple. It means the fiction should reveal its depth in layers.
