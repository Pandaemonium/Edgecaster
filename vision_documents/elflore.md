# Edgecaster Elflore Working Reference

## 1. Scope

This document captures what is already decided about elves and preserves the current design pool for what is still open.

It should be treated as a focused working reference, not a claim that all elven mechanics and institutions are settled.

## 2. Settled Anchors

These points are currently the strongest anchors:

- Elves are not humanoid.
- Physically, they resemble elk with fractal horns.
- Elves believe knowledge is power and do not share it freely.
- Information exchange is transactional: the "trade of whispers".
- Elven cults fill the gameplay and social role that religions often fill in other roguelikes.
- A character can belong to only one elven esoteric cult at a time.
- Cult progression likely grants one new ability per rank, with a max rank around 5 or 6.

## 3. Core Elven Feel

Elves should feel:

- esoteric
- secretive
- ceremonial
- exacting
- intellectually dangerous
- spiritually and magically specialized

They should not feel like generic wise forest people. Their mystery should come from structures of secrecy, transaction, ritual obligation, and strange forms of power.

## 4. The Trade Of Whispers

Knowledge is not treated as a public good. It is a store of power, leverage, identity, and obligation.

Important implications:

- information is traded, priced, pledged, hidden, encoded, or rationed
- secrets may function almost like currency
- an elf who gives away information freely is doing something notable
- social and magical systems can both be built around controlled disclosure

This should shape:

- dialogue
- quest structure
- cult advancement
- hidden doors and rites
- true-name mechanics
- bargains and oaths

## 5. Cult Structure And Gameplay Role

Elven cults are the main spiritual-esoteric tracks for elf-aligned play or interaction.

Current design direction:

- each cult has a distinct identity rather than being a cosmetic variant
- each cult offers a progression path
- each rank grants a specific new power, rite, or privilege
- a player can commit to one cult at a time
- cult identity should affect both mechanics and fiction

Open mechanical questions:

- whether switching cults is forbidden, costly, or possible through renunciation
- how rank is earned
- whether cult advancement is quest-based, rite-based, favor-based, or mixed
- how cult powers interact with chakra systems, runes, body swaps, and other major subsystems

## 6. Elven Concept Pool

The following motifs are in the current concept pool. They are ingredients, not promises. Not every item needs to become a cult, a power tree, or even explicit lore.

### Divination And Pattern Reading

- numerology
- corpse divination
- astrology
- dream omens
- ornithomancy and augury
- scrying by smoke, water, or metal
- listening to the wind

### Ritual, Vow, And Secrecy

- blood sacraments
- true names
- arcane vows
- steganography
- secret calendars
- secret melodies
- masks
- symbolic theater
- covens
- mantras

### Transformation, Purification, And Craft

- alchemy
- potions
- curses
- healing and purification
- genetic modification
- crafting lore
- rainbow powers

### Space, Memory, And Mind

- astral projection
- mind palaces
- mental maps
- caves and labyrinths
- secret lairs
- pilgrimages
- renunciation and emptiness

### Relics, Ancestors, And Sacred Objects

- reliquaries
- ancestor worship
- mandalas and rangolis
- fasting until an omen occurs

## 7. Guardrails

Do not:

- collapse elves into generic fantasy elf expectations
- make them simply "nature people"
- make every cult feel interchangeable
- treat secrecy as pure flavor with no social or mechanical consequence
- give away the best part of elven identity through exposition alone

Do:

- make information feel valuable
- let cult identity change how a character reads and navigates the world
- keep the non-humanoid body plan present in descriptions, art direction, and mechanical implications

## 8. Open Design Work

Still to be decided:

- cult names
- cult identities
- cult-specific ability ladders
- exact rank count
- how elves relate politically to the empire, demons, dwarves, birdfolk, and crocodilians
- what portions of elven lore are public rumor versus guarded truth

This file should narrow as decisions are made.
