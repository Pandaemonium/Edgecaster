EDGECASTER VISION (RUNE TRIALS + CHAKRAS)
========================================

Working title: Edgecaster: The Seals of a Fractal World
Length: ~2 pages

1) Premise and Fantasy
----------------------
Edgecaster is a roguelike where the world is a living fractal and the player is
a "caster of edges." You mend a failing world by reconstructing vast sealing
runes. Your body is a tree of chakras that defines the geometry of your magic.
To seal the land, you will sometimes have to steal or borrow a different body
to access the chakra shapes required by a specific rune.

The core fantasy: "math becomes myth." Players learn the grammar of patterns,
then use it to repair reality while surviving enemies in a world that is
continuously warped by corruption.


2) Design Pillars
-----------------
P1: Rune Trials are the spine of progression.
    - Each trial is a major encounter that changes the world when sealed.
    - The trial is a spatial puzzle under combat pressure.

P2: Chakras are the buildcraft system.
    - Your active chakras define your base generator.
    - Alignment and body composition are meaningful choices.

P3: The world feels coherent and strange.
    - Biomes, corruption, lairs, and factions are all derived from the same
      underlying field logic.


3) Core Loop
------------
1. Explore zones and gather bismuth, items, and intel.
2. Seek the next rune trial location and the tools to complete it.
3. Prepare: tune chakras, acquire tools, or swap bodies if needed.
4. Enter the trial zone and repair the damaged rune fragment.
5. Seal it with a Coherence Crystal.
6. The world state shifts; new challenges and routes open.

Roguelike loop: repeat with escalating complexity, higher corruption, and
more demanding rune geometry.


4) Rune Trial Experience
------------------------
Each trial is a large pre-existing rune that fills most of the screen. A single
missing chunk is chosen so it is "repairable" using a limited subset of
generators. The trial grants temporary abilities specific to the damage, and
snaps targeting to the canonical terminus to reduce frustration.

Key traits:
- The missing segment is subtle but distinct (pulsing).
- The player must stand at a highlighted root tile.
- The match is spatial and scored by overlap; auto-complete once aligned.
- The final seal requires a Coherence Crystal to lock the rune in place.

Trial tension: combat pressure while you build. Enemies spawn, terrain shifts,
and corruption surges as you delay.


5) Chakras as the Generator System
----------------------------------
Your active chakras form a tree of nodes and edges which becomes a custom
generator. Realignment lets you shift chakras within a limited radius (DEX
gated). This creates a "shape vocabulary" that the player can refine over time.

Design intent:
- Activate chakras to gain edges.
- Realign to change the generator's style.
- Resonances: specific configurations yield bonuses.
- Body swaps reset your available chakra graph but can unlock new shapes.


6) Body Swapping as Progression
-------------------------------
At least one trial requires a chakra geometry not available in the player's
current body. This encourages intentional possession and makes the body system
relevant to progression.

Example:
- Trial requires a generator with a steep branching angle and long lateral
  reach. The player's current body tree cannot produce it.
- A nearby legendary creature or NPC has the required body schema.
- You must possess it to complete the trial.

Body swap is not a one-off gimmick: it becomes a tactical tool and a form of
buildcraft. Your equipment and items remain with your old body, so swapping is
a trade-off.


7) World Evolution
------------------
Sealing runes alters the corruption field. As seals stabilize:
- Corruption retracts from key regions.
- Certain biomes or routes become safe.
- Faction attitudes may shift (some benefit from chaos).

Failure to seal in time may cause further distortion and enemy escalation.


8) Systems Touchpoints
----------------------
Chakra system -> Generator system -> Rune trials
Corruption field -> Biomes -> Faction zones -> Enemy pools
Quest journal -> Trial locations -> World map discovery
Items -> Chakra modulation -> Trial capability
Body swapping -> Chakra access -> Trial completion


9) Progression Structure
------------------------
Act 1: The Broken Seal
- Learn basic rune logic.
- First trial uses near-complete rune; small repair.
- Introduces Coherence Crystal and the inventor quest.

Act 2: The Fractured Network
- Multiple seals, each demanding different generators.
- Introduces body swap requirement for at least one trial.
- Corruption zones expand; lairs appear.

Act 3: The Great Binding
- Large, complex runes with multiple hazards.
- Seals impact factions and world topology.
- Final trial requires deep chakra mastery and/or rare bodies.


10) Aesthetic Direction
-----------------------
Tone: arcane and luminous, with "scientific occultism."
Visuals: fractal grids, glows, shimmering edges, subtle UI shimmer.
Sound: crystalline, resonant, with "mathematical" chimes on successes.

Language: “seals,” “coherence,” “resonance,” “anchors,” “iterations.”


11) What Success Looks Like
---------------------------
- Players say: "I learned the rune grammar."
- They can explain how their chakras change their magic.
- They feel their choices shape the world (corruption recedes).
- Trials are memorable and scary, not just puzzles.


12) Immediate Next Steps (for implementation)
----------------------------------------------
1. Solidify a "trial descriptor" schema: base pattern, missing edges, grants,
   root/terminus, match threshold, reward.
2. Build 3 handcrafted trial templates for early playtest.
3. Ensure chakra generator yields predictable baseline scaling.
4. Add at least one body swap "bridge trial" to prove the mechanic.
